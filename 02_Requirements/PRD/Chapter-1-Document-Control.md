## 1.2 Purpose

The Product Requirements Document (PRD) serves as the master reference for the DeployFix Lab project.

It defines:

- Product vision
- Product goals
- Functional requirements
- Non-functional requirements
- User requirements
- Product scope
- Engineering expectations
- Success criteria

Every engineering artifact produced throughout the project must align with this document.

## 1.10 Document Maintenance

The PRD is a living document.

Updates are permitted only when:

- A new feature is approved.
- Product scope changes.
- Requirements change.
- Architecture decisions significantly affect product behavior.
- Major releases introduce new capabilities.

All revisions must:

- Update the version history.
- Maintain requirement traceability.
- Preserve backward references where applicable.
- Be reviewed before approval.
